The guinea pig hair salon

So many kinds of cute.

Smooth, swirly, or long-haired: guinea pig breeds have wonderfully different coats. They are all domestic guinea pigs.

🐹

American

Short & smooth

The American has a short, smooth coat. A neat little hairdo with plenty of room for colorful markings.

🌀

Abyssinian

Wonderful whorls

This breed’s hair grows in little swirls called rosettes. Think of a guinea pig with a permanently playful hairdo!

🎀

Peruvian

Long & silky

The Peruvian is known for its long, silky hair. That flowing coat makes it look quite different from a short-haired cavy.

A color is not a breed.

Black, white, brown, cream, and mixed colors can appear on guinea pigs. Coat color alone does not tell you which breed a guinea pig is.

Breed descriptions: Merck Veterinary Manual.
